# Final Inter IIT Audit Report

## 1. Final Architecture
The architecture strictly enforces a one-way flow of information. The ML and Optimization pipeline remains 100% deterministic and isolated from the presentation layer. GenAI sits strictly at the end of the pipeline, functioning solely as a natural-language translator for SHAP values. 

## 2. Frozen Components
- **FP LightGBM Model**: Frozen (M7 training window 2015-2024).
- **P(play) LightGBM Model**: Frozen.
- **Expected FP**: Formula remains strict `Expected FP = P(play) * E[FP | play]`.
- **Candidate Pool**: Frozen (strictly pre-toss logic).
- **ILP Optimizer**: Frozen (Dream11 standard constraints).
- **SHAP Explanation**: Frozen.

## 3. Leakage Guarantees
All features in the canonical pipeline use `.shift(1)` after chronological sorting, ensuring target-match events, actual playing XIs, and post-match scorecard data cannot leak into the features. Evaluated heavily via `test_feature_leakage.py` and `test_target_leakage.py`.

## 4. Candidate Pool Methodology
Players are strictly eligible only if they have appeared in a T20 match *before* the target match date, ensuring no look-ahead bias is introduced during validation or testing.

## 5. FP Model
Predicts expected fantasy points conditional on the player playing. Uses rolling windows, EWMA spans, and career statistics.

## 6. P(play) Model
Predicts the probability a player will be in the final XI, accounting for historical participation, days since last match, and team-specific roles.

## 7. Expected FP
The final value function passed to the optimizer, balancing high-ceiling players with their likelihood of actually playing.

## 8. ILP
Integer Linear Programming (PuLP) solves for the optimal XI maximizing Expected FP subject to structural constraints (WKs, BATs, ARs, BOWLs, max 7 per team) and assigns C/VC multipliers.

## 9. SHAP
Provides the structural mathematical evidence for why the LightGBM models made their predictions. Identifies top positive and negative factors.

## 10. M10 Ablation Decision
Opponent and Venue contextual features were built and evaluated using walk-forward validation (M10). The improvement in FP MAE was negligible (0.06), and downstream metrics (Recall@11) were unchanged, while inference latency increased by ~400ms due to expanding joins. The decision was to **DROP** them. The M7 pipeline remains canonical.

## 11. M11 GenAI Architecture
- Takes verified structured SHAP JSON.
- Instructed strictly to not invent cricket facts.
- Output is captured and validated before rendering.

## 12. Guardrails
The validation layer (`_validate_and_fallback`) rejects any GenAI output that:
- Contains forbidden hallucination trigger words (e.g., `pitch`, `weather`, `toss`, `batting order`).
- Claims a player is "confirmed" to play when `p_play` < 0.99.
- Numerically conflicts with the expected FP values.
- Has malformed JSON structure.

## 13. Fallback
If validation fails, or if `GEMINI_API_KEY` is missing/errors out, the system deterministically defaults to the `human_readable_explanation` generated by the underlying M8 SHAP explainer.

## 14. Latency
- Core Inference: ~2.7s
- SHAP: ~0.1s
- GenAI Overhead: ~2.5s - 4.5s (Only occurs when explicitly enabled in the UI).
- UI Overhead: Minimal.

## 15. Test Results
- Total Tests: 43/43
- All leakage, optimization, tie-breaking, inference, UI, and GenAI guardrail tests passed.

## 16. Reproducibility
- 100/100 reproducibility achieved. Given the exact same date and teams, the candidate pool, features, predictions, ILP output, and SHAP factors remain completely byte-for-byte identical.

## 17. Known Limitations
- The model treats T20Is and Franchise T20s somewhat similarly without heavy distinct structural embeddings due to data constraints.
- Real-time toss information is intentionally excluded, meaning the model predicts a generic Pre-Toss expectation, which inherently has a lower ceiling than a post-toss model.
